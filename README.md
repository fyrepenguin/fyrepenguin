## 🧑‍💻 A human behind the browser

I shipped my first paid website in 2017 for a small studio that just wanted to be found. Almost a decade later I’m still chasing that feeling: making something that didn’t exist yesterday, and making it work for everyone.

I’ve built for banks, schools, newsrooms and startups, from design systems and fact-checking editors to data-dense banking grids. I like leaving codebases calmer than I found them: **typed, tested, accessible and fast.**

- 🔭 Currently building products at **[Dot Panda Labs](https://dotpandalabs.com)**
- 🏦 Recently untangled a big-bank React codebase for **Royal Bank of Canada** (via EPAM)
- 🎓 MSc Computer Science (AI & Data Science), University of East London
- ♿ Accessibility always. Not a ticket, a habit.
- 💬 Open to senior frontend roles & contracts

## 🌀 A little method. A little madness.

1. **Get curious.** Goals, people and wild ideas. The “why” before the “how.”
2. **Make a mess.** Sketches, prototypes and happy accidents. We find the direction together.
3. **Build it right.** Clean, accessible code, tested on real screens and tuned for speed.
4. **Let it fly.** A calm launch, a proper handover and support after go-live.
